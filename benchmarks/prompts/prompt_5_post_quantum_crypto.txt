### 3. Decryption Failure Probability (DFP) & Extreme Tail Bounds
In ML-KEM, decryption failure occurs when the rounding error term:
e_{dec} = \mathbf{s}^T \mathbf{e}' - \mathbf{e}^T \mathbf{s}' + e'' - c_v
exceeds q/4 for any polynomial coefficient.
(a) Prove why naive union bounds over the n = 256 polynomial coefficients produce grossly pessimistic DFP bounds, severely underestimating scheme security.
(b) Detail the exact Poisson summation / sum-of-independent-centered-binomial distribution method (D'Anvers, Vercauteren, Verbauwhede 2019) used in NIST FIPS 203: derive the exact discrete distribution of the coefficient inner product \langle \mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \rangle = \sum_{j=1}^{k} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a_{j,i} b_{j,i} and calculate the exact DFP bound \le 2^{-139} for ML-KEM-512 and \le 2^{-164} for ML-KEM-768.
(c) Analyze directional failure boosting attacks: explain how an adversary who observes a decryption failure can query crafted ciphertexts to reconstruct the secret key vector \mathbf{s} in polynomial time, and detail how the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform neutralizes this vulnerability.
